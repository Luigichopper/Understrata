# Understrata Art Bible

## Visual Identity & Pillars

1. **Readable Low-Poly Beauty**:
   - Faceted geometry with flat normals.
   - Large, clear silhouettes for rocks, arches, and structures.
   - Distinct, un-blurred faceted triangles.

2. **Crisp 16 px/m Pixel-Art Texel Density**:
   - 16x16 pixel textures per 1 meter face.
   - Nearest-neighbor magnification to retain crisp pixel edges without texture swimming.
   - World-space triplanar projection for seamless terrain textures.

3. **Lighting & Contrast**:
   - Deep, dark underground environments punctuated by vibrant colored light emitters (cyan/purple bioluminescent flora, orange torchlight).
   - Rich atmospheric depth with volumetrics and bloom.

4. **Overlay Model (Organic Terrain + Sharp Block Architecture)**:
   - Smooth, faceted organic terrain interpenetrates cleanly with 1-meter cubic building blocks.
